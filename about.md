Hi, I'm Kate! I've been baking cookies for as long as I could reach the counter. What started as holiday tins for neighbors and treats for school bake sales turned into so many requests that I decided to make it official.

**Kate's Cookies** is a one-person home bakery. Every batch is mixed, scooped, and baked by hand in my own kitchen, so things stay small on purpose. That means I can bake your order fresh, not days ahead, and I can make it exactly the way you want.

## What goes into every cookie

- **Real ingredients.** European-style butter, pure vanilla, and good chocolate. No mixes, no shortening.
- **Baked to order.** Your cookies come out of the oven within a day of pickup.
- **Rested dough.** Most of my doughs chill overnight for deeper flavor and better texture. It's worth the wait.
